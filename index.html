My Favourite thing I've read

The best thing I've read lately is a book from my childhood called "The Little Prince." I like to re-read it often, just out of pure enjoyment. This book is very significant to me because it's about growing up and becoming an adult, but also never forgetting your inner-child and imagination. I have also re-watched the movie several times, and for me it's the movie I put on when I'm not sure what to watch. There are many quotes from the book/movie that have stuck with me in life, and all the different characters the Prince encounters are representative of different kinds of people you may meet in the real world.
